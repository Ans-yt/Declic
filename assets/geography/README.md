# Administrative outlines

Source: DINUM / Etalab, derived from IGN ADMIN EXPRESS. Vintage 2025, generalised to 1,000 metres. These are orientation outlines, not cadastral or legal boundary measurements.

- https://etalab-datasets.geo.data.gouv.fr/contours-administratifs/2025/geojson/regions-1000m.geojson
- https://etalab-datasets.geo.data.gouv.fr/contours-administratifs/2025/geojson/departements-1000m.geojson
- Generator and source information: https://github.com/datagouv/contours-administratifs

Licence Ouverte / Open Licence 2.0: https://www.etalab.gouv.fr/licence-ouverte-open-licence/

Files include overseas territories in their actual geographic positions. Region dropdown entries follow the public Parcoursup catalogue. Boundary vintage and catalogue year are separate.

## Country context

`countries.geojson`: Natural Earth 1:110m Admin 0 countries, public domain. Retrieved 17 October 2026 from the project's maintainer repository. Geometry is unchanged; properties are reduced to ISO code, French/English names and label position to keep the download small (~262 KB).

- Source: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_admin_0_countries.geojson
- Terms: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

This is a coarse orientation basemap. Campus country labels are derived from published coordinates and these outlines, not an official institutional affiliation. French overseas territories remain part of France. Country outlines are noninteractive context; French departments use their separate, finer IGN/Etalab geometry.
